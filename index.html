PROJECT CELLULE

Exploring the Potential of AI in Healthcare

A project in the design phase exploring how artificial intelligence could help analyze and synthesize different categories of health data.

Our Objective

To investigate how combining health data could contribute to a more structured analysis of available information and the exploration of personalized care options.

Our Approach

The project is currently at the conceptual stage. The initial planned work will rely on synthetic data, without any personal patient information.

Responsible Design

Privacy, performance evaluation, human oversight, and compliance with regulatory requirements are key considerations in the project.

No clinical results or medical validation are claimed at this stage.
